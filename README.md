👋 About Me

I’m Subhro Mukherjee, a Data Science leader and hands-on Machine Learning practitioner based in Bengaluru, India.

Currently, I’m a Senior Manager – Data Science at Saks Global, working at the intersection of Machine Learning, Personalization, Generative AI, and Marketing Science.

My experience spans the complete ML lifecycle — from framing ambiguous business problems and developing models to deploying scalable ML systems and measuring their impact.

I enjoy working on problems where strong statistical reasoning, thoughtful ML engineering, and product thinking come together.

* 🔭 Currently working on: Personalization, recommendation systems, GenAI products, customer intelligence, and marketing measurement.
* 🧠 Areas of expertise: Applied ML, recommender systems, causal inference, experimentation, and ML system design.
* ⚙️ Engineering focus: Building reproducible, scalable, and production-ready ML pipelines.
* 🌱 Currently exploring: Advanced retrieval and ranking, Bayesian ML, LLM evaluation, and agentic AI.
* 🤝 Interested in: Applied research, ML engineering, personalization, and responsible AI systems.
* ⚽ Beyond work: Football, good food, and exploring new ideas.
